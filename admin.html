Roster

Everyone who should report each week. Untick Active for someone who has left: they drop out of the form and the report's missing lists, and their past submissions stay.

Project settings

You don't need to list projects: anything the team submits joins the dropdown on its own. Add a project here to set its default type (Claude / Non-Claude, filled in when someone picks it), or untick Active to hide it from the dropdown.

Projects the team has added

These are already in the dropdown. Choose Manage to set a default type or hide one, then save.
