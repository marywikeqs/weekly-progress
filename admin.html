Admin

Changes apply to the form as soon as you save.

Roster

Everyone who reports each week. Untick Active for someone who has left: they drop off the form and out of the report's counts, and their past updates stay.

Report email

The report is emailed once a week: as soon as everyone on the roster has sent their update, or on Monday at 9 AM with whoever is in if someone is still missing. Separate several addresses with commas.

    Projects

    You don't need to list projects: anything the team sends joins the dropdown on its own. Add one here to set its default type (filled in when someone picks it), or untick Active to hide it from the dropdown.

    Added by the team. Already in the dropdown. Choose Manage to set a type or hide one.

      Other work

      The kinds of work people can pick in the form's Other work section. They can also type something else, and what they type stays pickable afterwards.

      Screenshot instructions

      Shown above the screenshot upload. Name the screen and the date range so everyone's screenshots match.
